Threat Detection Pillars

What FraudLens AI Watches For

Designed to catch common signs of fake shopping websites and risky links before you complete checkout.

Domain Age Check

Identifies brand-new domains that are often spun up quickly for temporary scam stores.

Discount Trap Detection

Highlights extreme price cuts and aggressive ad bait that lure buyers into risky payments.

Store Policy & Contact

Checks for visible return/refund terms and reachable support details on the storefront.

Watchlist & Risk Scoring

Matches against reported scam domains and calculates a combined risk score with clear guidance.

Buyer Awareness

Smart Habits Before You Buy Online

Don't Rush Flash Deals

Fake ad stores rely on urgency and huge discounts so buyers skip checking domain trust.

Verify Return Policies

Look for clear refund, return, and customer care details before sharing payment info.

Flag Suspicious Stores

Report fake stores once signed in to help protect other shoppers checking similar links.
